// 01 — Who we are

Engineering discipline, research mindset.

Team G-SOCIETY is a student-led engineering collective within the Department of Computer Science at the University of Eldoret. We operate like a small software studio: scoped sprints, code review, documentation, and demo days. Our work spans academic research projects, campus systems, and competitive security challenges.

Ship real software

Sprint-based delivery with Git workflows, peer review and CI — not throwaway coursework.

Security by default

Every system we deploy is threat-modelled, tested against OWASP, and hardened before handover.

Research grounded

Projects tie back to coursework and papers — from ML models to distributed systems.

Mentorship chain

Finalists mentor second-years. Knowledge is documented, not gate-kept.
